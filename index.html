01 / About

I put AI apps and agents onto real cloud and data estates, governed and measured.

In 30 seconds

I’m a Solutions Architect for Cloud, Data & AI at Revantage Global in Singapore. I’ve spent 10+ years building enterprise platforms on Azure and AWS. Today I run governed AI in production: an Azure AI Foundry gateway, and AI agents on Databricks, Snowflake, and Sigma.

In 2 minutes

I started in infrastructure: vRealize automation across 1000+ VMs at Cognizant, then cloud migrations, landing zones, and Terraform/GitLab pipelines at Accenture in Bangalore and Singapore.

At Revantage Global I moved from Senior DevOps Engineer, designing Hub-and-Spoke architectures, IaC, Kubernetes, and observability, to Solutions Architect for Cloud, Data & AI. I implemented Azure AI Foundry as the gateway that manages, monitors, and governs our AI solutions, and I design AI agents on Databricks, Snowflake, and Sigma that cut manual analysis for Ops and business teams. I also run evals and operational-readiness checks, and I evaluate Cursor and MCP with security and configuration first.

Outside work I build open-source labs like an LLM auto-router, a production-grade RAG, and MCP tools, and I enter AI hackathons to test ideas before they reach production.
